1. Product Strategy
Double down on Men’s Street Footwear: It is the top product in 4 of 5 regions and the single biggest revenue driver. Increase inventory, marketing spend, and product variation in this category.
Urgently review Women’s Athletic Footwear: It is dead last in every single region. Conduct customer research to understand whether the issue is pricing, design, awareness, or distribution.
Investigate Men’s Apparel pricing: It has decent unit volumes but the lowest revenue — suggesting aggressive discounting. Review the pricing architecture for this category.

2. Channel Optimization
Invest heavily in the online channel: It already has the highest margin (46.4%) and the most units sold. A pricing optimization strategy for online could significantly grow revenue without any increase in volume.
Review In-store operating costs: In-store generates the most revenue but the lowest margin. Physical retail costs like rent and staffing are compressing profits — audit store-level profitability.
Limit Outlet dependency: Outlet sales drive volume but train customers to expect discounts, which damages long-term brand value and margin.

3. Regional Focus
Increase investment in the South: The South has the highest operating margin (46.7%) of any region but ranks 4th in revenue. It is underinvested relative to its profitability potential.
Investigate the West’s low margins: Despite leading in revenue at $269.9M, the West has the lowest margin at 39.7%. Heavy discounting or high local costs may be the cause.
Launch a Midwest growth campaign: The Midwest is last in revenue and has no standout strengths. A targeted regional marketing campaign focused on top products could unlock growth.

4. Demand Forecasting
Prepare heavily for Q3: Q3 is the peak quarter in both 2020 and 2021. Stock inventory early, run marketing campaigns in Q2 to build momentum, and ensure supply chain readiness by June.
Launch promotions in Q4: Q4 sees a consistent revenue dip after the summer peak. Strategic promotions and holiday campaigns can cushion this seasonal decline.
Build digital resilience: The 2020 Q4 crash to $27.5M was driven by COVID-19 physical retail disruptions. A stronger online channel acts as a buffer against future retail disruptions.

5. Retailer & Partner Management
Reduce dependence on West Gear and Foot Locker: Two retailers represent 51% of all revenue a significant concentration risk. Develop strategies to grow Amazon, Kohl’s and Walmart.
Investigate Walmart’s underperformance: Walmart is the world’s largest retailer but Adidas’ worst-performing partner. Review product mix, placement, pricing, and marketing support provided to Walmart.
Grow Amazon: Amazon’s online-native model aligns perfectly with the finding that the Online channel has the highest margins. Strengthening the Amazon partnership could accelerate the online channel strategy.
